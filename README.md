# wmSpaceWeather: a space weather monitor

### Summary

wmSpaceWeather is a [Window Maker](https://www.windowmaker.org/) dock
application which is space weather monitor.


### Description

The monitor has various leds, the top two show the relativistic
electron flux levels. The first corresponds to energy greater than 800
KeV, the second to energies greater than 2 MeV. The three next leds
show the relativistic proton flux levels in three energy channels,
greater than 10 MeV, greater than 50 MeV and greater than 100 MeV (the
displayed channels can be easily modified in getspaceweather.py).
Next comes the [solar flare X-ray
class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_flare). The bottom of the
monitor displays the [Kp
index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-index), updated every 3 hours
over the last 24 hours (horizontal axis) and color-coded from 0 to 9
(vertical axis).
